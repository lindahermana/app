## 🆘 If Things Go Wrong

### Low Downloads (<100 in Week 1)
**Fix:**
- Double TikTok effort (10x/day temporarily)
- Ask friends to share
- Post in more communities
- Run small Instagram ad ($20)
- Reach out to micro-influencers

### Bad Reviews (<4.0 stars)
**Fix:**
- Respond within 1 hour
- Fix bugs immediately
- Push update within 48 hours
- Email reviewers when fixed
- Ask happy users to review

### TikTok Not Growing
**Fix:**
- Study viral videos in niche
- Copy format (not content)
- Use trending audio
- Post at peak times (11am, 5pm EST)
- Engage more (1 hour/day)

### App Rejected by Apple
**Fix:**
- Read rejection reason carefully
- Fix issue
- Resubmit within 24 hours
- Usually minor issues (screenshots, description)
- Don't panic, happens often
