# App Store Strategy - Split Decision

## App Store Optimization (ASO)

### App Name
**Primary:** Split Decision
**Subtitle:** Help Me Decide Everything

### Keywords (100 characters max)
`decision,poll,vote,choose,help,indecisive,decide,friends,social,quiz,survey,outfit,food,dating`

### Short Description (170 characters)
Can't decide? Let your friends choose for you! Create quick polls for any decision. Fashion, food, dating - never overthink again. 🎯

### Long Description

**Hook:**
😰 Spent 20 minutes choosing an outfit? Stared at a menu for too long? Can't decide which shoes to buy?

**Solution:**
Split Decision is here to save you from decision paralysis! 

**How it works:**
1️⃣ Create a decision (What to wear? Where to eat? Should I text them?)
2️⃣ Add 2-4 options with photos
3️⃣ Share with friends or post publicly  
4️⃣ Watch votes come in real-time
5️⃣ Follow the crowd (or don't, no judgment!)

**Perfect for:**
👗 Fashion decisions (outfit of the day)
🍕 Food choices (restaurant, recipes)
💬 Dating dilemmas (should I text first?)
🛍️ Shopping comparisons (which to buy)
✈️ Travel planning (destination, hotel)
💼 Career choices (job offers, side hustles)

**Features:**
✨ Quick poll creation (< 30 seconds)
📸 Add photos to your options
🔥 See real-time voting results  
⏱️ Optional decision timer
📊 Decision history & analytics
🔒 Public or private decisions
🎨 Beautiful, shareable decision cards
🏆 See the winning option instantly

**Free vs Premium:**
🆓 Free: 10 decisions per month
👑 Premium: Unlimited decisions, no ads, advanced analytics, custom themes

**Why Split Decision?**
- Stop overthinking everything
- Get instant feedback from friends
- Make decisions confidently
- Join a community of fellow over-thinkers
- Turn decision-making into a fun social experience

**Join thousands of people who can't decide on their own** (and that's okay!) 🎉

Whether you're chronically indecisive or just want a second opinion, Split Decision has your back.

Download now and never waste time overthinking again!

**Follow us on TikTok: @splitdecisionapp**
See real decisions, share yours, and join the community!

## Launch Timing Strategy

### Soft Launch (TestFlight)
**Week 1-4:**
- Beta with 100-500 users
- Gather feedback
- Fix critical bugs
- Optimize onboarding

### App Store Launch
**Best Days:** Tuesday-Thursday (more App Store visibility)
**Avoid:** Friday-Sunday (less App Store traffic)
**Optimal Time:** 9 AM EST (catches East Coast morning, West Coast early AM)

**Month:** 
- Avoid: Major holidays, Black Friday, December (crowded)
- Good: January (New Year resolutions), September (back to school), March-April (spring)

## App Store Reviews Strategy

**Getting Reviews:**
1. **In-App Prompt:** After user creates 3rd decision (positive experience point)
2. **Email Campaign:** Ask beta testers specifically
3. **TikTok CTA:** "Leave a review and I'll feature your decision"
4. **Friends & Family:** First 20-30 reviews from trusted circle

**Review Response Template:**

For Positive Reviews (5 stars):
```
Thanks so much! 🎉 We're so happy Split Decision is helping you make decisions. Follow us @splitdecisionapp on TikTok to see your decision featured! 

- Split Decision Team
```

For Constructive Reviews (3-4 stars):
```
Thanks for the feedback! We're working on [specific feature mentioned]. Update coming soon! If you have more ideas, email us at support@splitdecision.app

- Split Decision Team  
```

For Negative Reviews (1-2 stars):
```
We're sorry you had this experience. We'd love to make it right. Please email support@splitdecision.app and we'll help ASAP.

- Split Decision Team
```

**Response Time:** Within 24 hours for all reviews
