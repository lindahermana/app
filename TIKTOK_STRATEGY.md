## 🎥 LOW-EFFORT PRODUCTION GUIDE

### Equipment Needed
- iPhone (any model with decent camera)
- Natural lighting (window)
- Optional: Ring light ($20)
- Optional: Phone tripod ($15)

### Video Style Guidelines
✅ DO:
- Film vertically (9:16)
- Use text overlays
- Keep 15-30 seconds
- Show genuine reactions
- Use trending sounds
- Post consistently

❌ DON'T:
- Over-produce
- Use complex editing
- Film in loud places
- Make videos too long
- Skip posting days
- Ignore comments

### Quick Filming Tips
1. Batch film 20 videos per week (1 day = 1 week of content)
2. Film at window for natural lighting
3. Use CapCut for quick editing
4. Save drafts to post throughout week
5. Film multiple takes, pick the best
6. Reuse concepts that work well

### Engagement Strategy
- Reply to ALL comments in first hour
- Ask questions in captions
- Use polls in stories
- Go live once per week
- Engage with similar creators
- Cross-post to Instagram Reels
