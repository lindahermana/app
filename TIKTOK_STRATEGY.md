# TikTok Content Strategy - Split Decision

## Daily Content Plan (5 Videos/Day)

### Video Format Template

**Equipment Needed:**
- iPhone (whatever you have)
- Natural lighting (window is fine)
- Built-in screen recording
- TikTok app only (no fancy editing software)

**Video Length:** 15-60 seconds each

---

## Monday - Friday Schedule

### Video 1 (8 AM): Morning Decision
**Type:** Help Me Decide
**Example Topics:**
- "Which outfit for work today?"
- "What should I have for breakfast?"
- "Should I go to the gym or sleep in?"

**Script Template:**
```
[Show face] "Good morning! I can't decide..."
[Screen record] Shows the poll in app
[Back to face] "Help me out!"
[Text overlay] "Download Split Decision to make your own"
```

### Video 2 (12 PM): Lunch/Mid-Day Decision
**Type:** Help Me Decide
**Example Topics:**
- "Where should I eat lunch?"
- "Should I take a break or keep working?"
- "Which playlist for my workout?"

**Script Template:**
```
[Screen record only] Shows creating the decision
[Speed up to 1.5x] Makes it snappy
[End with results screen]
[Text overlay] Link in bio
```

### Video 3 (3 PM): Community Showcase
**Type:** Reaction Content
**Example Topics:**
- React to wild decisions others made
- "This person used Split Decision for..."
- Showcase interesting polls

**Script Template:**
```
[Duet or Stitch format]
[React to another user's decision]
"Wait, they let the internet decide THAT?! 💀"
[Show the results]
"Actually though... not bad"
```

### Video 4 (6 PM): Evening Decision + Reveal
**Type:** Decision Reveal
**Example Topics:**
- "You chose my dinner... here's what happened"
- "Update: The [choice] was perfect/terrible"
- "They really made me choose THIS"

**Script Template:**
```
[Show original poll from earlier]
"So you guys chose..."
[Dramatic pause]
[Show the actual thing/outcome]
"I can't believe you made me..."
```

### Video 5 (9 PM): Relatable/Trending
**Type:** Trend-jacking
**Example Topics:**
- Use trending audio with decision-making angle
- "POV: You can never make decisions"
- Storytime about a past decision gone wrong/right

**Script Template:**
```
[Trending audio]
[Relatable situation]
[Quick shot of app]
"This app literally saves me every day"
[Natural, not salesy]
```

## Content Pillars Breakdown

### Pillar 1: "Help Me Decide" (40% of content)
**Goal:** Show app in action, create FOMO

**Topics that ALWAYS work:**
1. Fashion (OOTD, which shoes, accessories)
2. Food (restaurants, recipes, snacks)
3. Dating (text message replies, date ideas, "should I text")
4. Shopping (which to buy, keep or return)
5. Hair/Beauty (new hairstyle, makeup look)
6. Travel (destination, hotel, activities)

**Hook Examples:**
- "I'm letting strangers decide..."
- "Help me before I overthink this..."
- "Can't choose so you're choosing for me"
- "POV: You're indecisive AF"

### Pillar 2: "Decision Reveals" (30% of content)
**Goal:** Show outcomes, create engagement loop

**Format:**
1. Reminder of original decision (3 sec)
2. Build suspense (2 sec)
3. Show the outcome (10-15 sec)
4. React authentically

**Best Performing:**
- Fashion reveals (showing the outfit)
- Food reveals (tasting the food)
- "Regret" reveals (funny failures)
- "They were right!" (validating the choice)

### Pillar 3: "Reaction Content" (20% of content)
**Goal:** Build community, encourage UGC

**Types:**
- Duet with user decisions
- Stitch reactions to wild choices
- Compilation of best decisions of the week
- Reading comments on past decisions

### Pillar 4: "Relatable/Educational" (10% of content)
**Goal:** Build trust, show personality

**Topics:**
- Why you can't make decisions (psychology)
- Tips for overthinkersStorytime: Worst/best decision you made
- Behind the scenes of your content creation

## UGC (User Generated Content) Strategy

**Goal:** Get users to create content WITH your app

**Tactics:**

1. **Challenge Series:**
   - "24 Hours Following Split Decision"
   - "Let my Split Decision picks go viral"
   - "Split Decision Roulette" (random decisions)

2. **Stitch/Duet Prompts:**
   - "Show me your Split Decision results"
   - "Duet this and make a decision with me"
   - "Stitch with your wildest decision"

3. **Engagement Bait:**
   - "Comment your decision and I'll make it"
   - "Most liked comment = my next decision"
   - "Reply to this with your dilemma"

4. **Success Stories:**
   - Feature users who made interesting decisions
   - Highlight the community
   - Create a "Decision of the Week"

## Low-Effort Production Tips

**Keep it SIMPLE:**

1. **Lighting:** Window light is enough
2. **Background:** Clean wall or your regular space
3. **Editing:** Use TikTok's built-in tools only
4. **Music:** Trending sounds (no original score needed)
5. **Effects:** Minimal (text overlay max)
6. **Script:** Bullet points, not word-for-word
7. **Takes:** 2-3 max, embrace imperfection
8. **Duration:** 15-30 seconds ideal

**Batch Recording:**
- Record all 5 videos in 1 hour
- Monday: Record M/T/W content
- Thursday: Record Th/F/S content
- Schedule posts in advance

---

## Red Flags to Avoid

❌ **Don't:**
- Over-edit (kills authenticity)
- Post only promotional content
- Ignore comments
- Copy others exactly
- Give up before 90 days
- Buy followers/engagement
- Use banned/copyrighted audio

✅ **Do:**
- Be genuine and relatable
- Engage with your audience
- Show your personality
- Test and iterate
- Stay consistent
- Track what works
- Enjoy the process

## The Secret Sauce

**Why This Strategy Works:**

1. **Volume:** 5x daily = more lottery tickets
2. **Variety:** Multiple content types = broader reach
3. **Relatability:** Everyone struggles with decisions
4. **Shareability:** People tag friends for opinions
5. **Utility:** App actually solves a problem
6. **Community:** Built-in engagement loop
7. **Authenticity:** Low-effort = more genuine

**The real key:** CONSISTENCY over perfection.

Better to post 5 "good enough" videos daily than wait for 1 "perfect" video weekly.
