# Split Decision - iOS App

## Overview

**Split Decision** is an iOS app that helps people make everyday decisions by creating quick polls for their friends. Perfect for the chronically indecisive, this app turns decision-making into a social, shareable experience.

## The Concept

Have you ever stood in your closet for 20 minutes trying to decide what to wear? Or stared at a menu unable to choose? Split Decision solves this by letting your friends decide for you.

**How it works:**
1. Create a decision with 2-4 options
2. Share with friends or post publicly
3. Watch votes come in real-time
4. Follow the winning choice (or don't, we won't judge 😉)

## Why This Will Succeed

### 1. **Unsaturated Niche**
- No dominant player in "social decision helper" apps
- Poll apps exist, but none are TikTok-native or viral-focused
- First mover advantage in combining decision-making + social sharing

### 2. **Universal Problem**
- Decision fatigue affects everyone
- Analysis paralysis is real (especially for Gen Z/Millennials)
- People trust crowd wisdom and social validation

### 3. **Built-in Virality**
- Every use case = potential TikTok content
- Users create content BY using the app
- FOMO drives downloads (fear of missing out on decisions)
- Network effects: more users = more decisions = more value

### 4. **TikTok-Ready Content**
- Low-effort videos (screen record + reaction)
- Highly relatable content (everyone makes decisions)
- Natural duet/stitch opportunities
- Series potential ("Day X of letting Split Decision control my life")

### 5. **Multiple Revenue Streams**
- **Free Tier:** 10 decisions/month with ads
- **Premium:** $4.99/month or $29.99/year (unlimited, no ads, private polls, analytics)
- **Sponsored Decisions:** Brands pay to sponsor popular categories
- **Affiliate:** Fashion/food/shopping decisions → affiliate links
