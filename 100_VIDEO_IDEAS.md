## Video Formulas (Plug & Play)

### Formula 1: Decision → Results → Reaction
```
Hook: "Help me decide [thing]"
Middle: Show poll with options
End: Reveal results + your reaction
CTA: "Download Split Decision"
```

### Formula 2: Before & After
```
Hook: "You guys chose [option]..."
Middle: Show original poll
End: Show the outcome
CTA: "Link in bio"
```

### Formula 3: Series
```
Hook: "Day [X] of letting Split Decision control my life"
Middle: Show today's decision
End: "Tomorrow I'm deciding [next thing]"
CTA: "Follow for part [X+1]"
```

### Formula 4: Reaction
```
Hook: "This person used Split Decision for [wild thing]"
Middle: React to their decision
End: "What would you do?"
CTA: "Tag someone who needs this"
```

### Formula 5: Educational
```
Hook: "Why you can't make decisions"
Middle: Psychology fact or relatable struggle
End: "This app helps"
CTA: "Try it yourself"
```

## Pro Tips

✅ **Do:**
- Post consistently (5x daily minimum)
- Engage with every comment (first hour)
- Use decision outcomes as content (2 videos from 1 decision)
- Batch record (5 videos in 1 hour)
- Save every video idea that pops up
- Respond to trends within 24 hours
- Track which formats perform best
- Collaborate with other creators

❌ **Don't:**
- Over-edit (kills authenticity)
- Ignore your audience
- Post only promotional content
- Copy others exactly
- Give up after slow videos
- Buy fake engagement
- Use banned audio
