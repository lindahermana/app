# 🎯 Split Decision - Executive Summary

## The Opportunity

**Problem:** Decision fatigue affects millions. People spend hours overthinking simple choices (what to wear, where to eat, should I text them).

**Solution:** Split Decision - an iOS app that turns decision-making into a fun, social experience by letting friends vote on your choices.

**Market:** Unsaturated niche. No dominant TikTok-native decision-making app exists.

## The App

### Core Concept
Create quick polls (2-4 options) → Share with friends → Watch votes in real-time → Follow the winner

### Key Features
- ✅ Quick decision creation (< 30 seconds)
- ✅ Category-based (Fashion, Food, Dating, Shopping, Travel)
- ✅ Real-time voting
- ✅ Public/private polls
- ✅ Decision history
- ✅ Beautiful, shareable cards

### Monetization
**Free Tier:** 10 decisions/month + ads
**Premium:** $4.99/mo or $29.99/yr (unlimited, no ads, private polls, analytics)

## Why This Will Work

### 1. Built-in Virality
Every app use = potential TikTok content
- User opens app (screen record)
- Creates decision
- Gets results
- Shows outcome
= Natural viral loop

### 2. Universal Problem
- Everyone makes decisions daily
- Decision paralysis is real (Barry Schwartz research)
- Gen Z/Millennials especially struggle
- People trust crowd wisdom

### 3. TikTok-Native Strategy
- Low-effort content (screen record + reaction)
- 5 videos per day = 1,825/year
- Relatable, shareable format
- Natural duet/stitch opportunities

### 4. Multiple Revenue Streams
- Subscriptions (70%)
- Advertising (20%)
- Sponsorships (10%)
= Diversified, resilient business

### 5. Network Effects
More users → More decisions → More value → More sharing → More users
